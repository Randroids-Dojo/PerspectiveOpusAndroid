## Full description

The Opus, a great symphony, was written on a page and performed on a stage. Its notes have scattered. Quaver, the last little note, can walk on both.

Perspective Opus is a puzzle platformer played in two worlds at once. Tap to turn the world at any moment, even in mid-air:

THE SCORE is a hand-inked parchment page. Depth disappears, so anything that lines up is joined. A ledge at the front and a ledge at the back become one path.

THE STAGE is a lit diorama. Depth is real. Walls that block the page are only as deep as they look, so you can walk round them.

Every puzzle needs both. Cross a chasm on the page, slip behind a pillar on the stage, bounce on timpani, press piano keys that raise golden bars, ride music stands and stay clear of the Discords.

• Six movements, from a sunrise meadow to a misty lake, an autumn village, moonlit gardens, a clocktower organ and a concert hall at sunset
• Forty-two lost notes, and every one you find brings an instrument back to the music
• An original score, arranged twice for each movement: intimate on the page, full and wide on the stage. Turning the world crossfades between them without missing a beat
• Touch, controller and keyboard support
• No ads, no purchases, no account, no data collected

A game by toyboxes.games. Also playable in the browser at perspective-opus.vercel.app.
